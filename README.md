# Retail Business Performance Analysis

A Power BI portfolio project analysing retail sales, customers, products, profitability, and territory performance.

The project uses SQL reporting views, a Power BI data model, DAX measures, and interactive dashboards to turn transactional data into business insights.

## Dashboard Overview

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of overall business performance.

Key KPIs:
- Total Revenue: $109.85M
- Total Orders: 31K
- Total Customers: 19K
- Average Order Value: $3.49K
- Estimated Gross Profit: $12.56M
- Estimated Gross Margin: 11.43%

Visuals include:
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Sales Channel
- Revenue by Territory

### 2. Product & Customer Analysis
Focuses on product and customer performance.

Analysis includes:
- Top 10 Products by Revenue
- Revenue by Product Category
- Top 10 Customers by Revenue
- Revenue by Customer Type

### 3. Sales & Territory Analysis
Compares performance across different sales territories.

Analysis includes:
- Revenue by Territory
- Orders by Territory
- Average Order Value by Territory
- Active Customers by Territory

## Key Insights

- Offline sales generate approximately $80.49M compared with $29.36M from online sales.
- Southwest is the highest-revenue territory at approximately $24.18M.
- Bikes are the strongest product category, generating approximately $94.65M.
- Mountain-200 Black, 38 is the highest-revenue product at approximately $4.40M.
- Store customers contribute around 73% of customer revenue.
